# Prepare a first paid maintenance information review

The [service description](../../docs/WORK_WITH_ME.md) is the client entry point. These copyable documents
make a bounded assignment concrete; blank prices, dates and names must be agreed for the actual engagement.
No paying customer, accepted plant result or market-tested price is represented here.

1. Use [FIRST_CONVERSATION.md](FIRST_CONVERSATION.md) to establish the recurring problem, recipient and buyer.
2. Fill [SCOPE.md](SCOPE.md) with exact source limits, deliverables, responsibilities and acceptance.
3. Use [QUOTE_WORKSHEET.md](QUOTE_WORKSHEET.md) privately to estimate all work before quoting the scope.
4. Deliver through the client's existing approved records system and use [REVIEW_AND_NEXT_JOB.md](REVIEW_AND_NEXT_JOB.md)
   to record receipt, next revision, measured effort and any follow-on demand.

Demonstrate a result with the existing [markup return](../pid-markup-review/README.md),
[equipment reconciliation](../maintenance-intake/README.md) or [cross-sheet example](../pid-path-review/README.md).
Use the example appropriate to the buyer's problem, then inspect a permitted sample of their own records.
Examples remain public teaching evidence; do not imply that fictional work came from a client.

The recommended starting scope is a commercial hypothesis to test, not evidence of willingness to pay.
Ask about the last actual occurrence, its current workaround and the person who could fund the review.
Record a declined scope and its reason as carefully as an accepted one. Product requirements should follow
recurring paid work, reviewed user behavior and receiving-system effort. No outreach is sent by this package.

This decision-first method is consistent with NIST's discussion of
[different information needs for maintenance decisions](https://www.nist.gov/publications/selecting-optimal-data-creating-informed-maintenance-decisions-manufacturing).
NIST also documents how [maintenance work-order data quality can distort analysis](https://www.nist.gov/publications/impact-data-quality-maintenance-work-order-analysis-case-study-historical-hvac).
Those sources support investigating the selected evidence; they do not validate this toolkit or prove demand.
The SBA's [business planning resources](https://www.sba.gov/counseling/plan-your-business/) provide a separate
starting point for market research, costs and business planning.
