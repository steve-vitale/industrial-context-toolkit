# First conversation: understand one recurring job

Problem / site or unit / date: ____
Person who uses the result: ____
Person who can authorize spending: ____
Next actual work order, drawing return or project milestone: ____

- Describe the last time this problem occurred. Which decision or job was delayed?
- How did the team find and check the records? Who did the preparation, review and corrections?
- What was missing or wrong, and how was it discovered? Which evidence still exists?
- What would the recipient need in hand to move that specific job forward?
- Which records can be shared in an approved private location? What needs a walkdown or another owner?
- Who already performs this work? Would a scoped outside review complement their work?
- What would make a small paid assessment worth approving? Who reviews the quote and deliverable?
- What would make you decline, narrow or defer this assignment?
