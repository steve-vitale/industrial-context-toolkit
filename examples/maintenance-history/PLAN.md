# Maintenance history to a reviewable asset packet

Goal opened 2026-11-08. This is implementation and validation work, not a claim
that maintenance performance or plant applicability has been established.

## Deliver one useful job

Given explicitly mapped asset, work-order and optional parts/labor exports,
produce an offline manager brief and asset dossiers: selected-record chronology,
recurring-work review candidates, associated source evidence, unknown meanings,
coverage gaps and specific follow-up actions. Retain exact originals, row
locators and earlier results when corrected or replacement exports arrive.
Accept two ordinary export layouts through configuration rather than site code.
Reuse the existing intake, provenance and revision tools where appropriate.

The public AMPS acquisition already checks relationships but performs no semantic
maintenance investigation. Read its original records and primary publisher
definitions before choosing interpretations. Unknown meanings remain unknown.
Repeated work is not proven repeat failure; linked parts are not installed parts;
missing rows do not establish absence. Costs, hours and net consumption require
confirmed units, signs and correction semantics. Keep the request-date cohort
distinct from work performed during that week and from a historical snapshot.

## Development, baseline and reserved evaluation

Use the already exposed AMPS 2024-01-01 inclusive to 2024-01-08 exclusive request
cohort for development. Compare a simple raw-asset grouping/count baseline with
the proposed source-qualified packet on the same inputs. Freeze explicit expected
behaviour and negative controls before implementation, then record the final
method and input identities before opening reserved results.

Preselect the nonoverlapping AMPS request-date interval 2024-01-15 inclusive to
2024-01-22 exclusive for public validation, with the existing publisher tables,
explicit projections and deterministic related-ID queries. Do not inspect its
record contents before the method freeze. Retain acquisition failures rather
than substituting another interval. This is another cohort from one municipal
source, not an independent industrial plant or a complete asset history.

Test wrong joins, duplicate identities, ambiguous statuses/dates/flags,
parts returns/corrections, partial coverage and source revisions. Check exact
source locators and the substantive result, not only successful execution.
Keep source terms and redistribution limits explicit; use original acquisition
pins and redistributable fictional fixtures for offline CI when required.

## Finish criteria and stopping rule

- A documented own-input command creates readable, source-linked packets.
- Both configured layouts, portable replay and a revised-input cycle work.
- Matched baseline, deliberately misleading controls and every reserved outcome
  are retained with their actual evidence and limitations.
- Agent/manager guides and a bounded consulting assignment explain use and scope.
- Appropriate regressions and exact-final-head CI finish with skips reported.

Do not expand into native CAD, new reader research, alert services, live CMMS
connections or a general platform. A reserved failure remains an outcome; it
does not require another experimental campaign to finish this goal. Root cause,
MTBF, savings, repair effectiveness and organizational acceptance are outside
these software/public-data checks.
