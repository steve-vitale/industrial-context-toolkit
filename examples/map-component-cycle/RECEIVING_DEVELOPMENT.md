## Why continue into a managed service?

The portable packet already provides useful context, source-linked answers,
drafts, history and an independent exit. A managed service must add continuing
authenticated access, controlled source retention/withdrawal, revision custody,
durable audit and accountable maintenance at the required scale. The customer
should be able to see and query the same facts and uncertainty after receiving,
without entering them again, and export them again if they leave.

Company document, CAD and maintenance systems can keep their authoritative
records. Receiving context does not transfer their permissions or make a draft
an accepted engineering decision. SharePoint access supplies reachable records;
the optional MCP interface supplies typed item/fact/relationship/history queries
over the retained model. It is useful where that structure is needed and is not
required merely to read a SharePoint file.
