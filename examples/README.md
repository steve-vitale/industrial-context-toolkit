# Worked engineering examples

Choose your job in the [canonical example map](../docs/README.md#engineering-work-examples).
For a first contribution with no installation, use the [first-question guide](first-pilot/README.md).

Tools accept your inputs in their documented contracts. Tutorials use public or fictional fixtures to show what
to substitute. Case studies and evaluations retain results for their particular sources. Each guide states its
role, prerequisites, output and review boundary; the [workflow map](../docs/WORKFLOWS.md) owns format connections.

For executable first steps, use [reproduce a workflow](../docs/REPRODUCE_A_WORKFLOW.md).
It routes maintenance reconciliation, [asset-history investigation](maintenance-history/README.md), markup returns, exact ports, cross-sheet paths, drawing bundles,
knowledge revisions and recorded batch calculations to their existing commands and expected findings.
For equipment-data proposals and native drawing evidence, use the
[CAD handoff](cad-handoff/README.md) and [qualification index](cad-handoff/qualification/README.md).
Manual calculation/automation worksheets and scope/quote/receiving forms live in
[consulting-start](consulting-start/README.md); they are methods, not additional executable integrations.

Inside an example, `inputs/` or `source/` retains the basis; `worked/`, `expected/` or named results retain
actual/expected outputs as its guide states. `PLAN`, `ACCEPTANCE`, `FREEZE`, inventories and historical failures
document the experiment when supplied. Do not infer a file's authority from its folder name alone, regenerate
a key from the method under test, or delete old evidence because a newer result passes.
