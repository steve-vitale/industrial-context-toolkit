# Work with me on a plant information problem

If maintenance records disagree, drawings are missing, or an engineering question takes too long to reconstruct,
start with one equipment system and one decision someone needs to make.

[Connect with me on LinkedIn and message me there](https://www.linkedin.com/in/steve-vitale/).
Describe the problem, the kinds of records you have and who needs the result. Keep company documents private;
the first conversation does not require an upload or a GitHub issue.

Agree reading coverage and engineering review before claiming completion. |

The open-source tools provide starting points for your own records. A scoped engagement adds investigation,
record preparation, field verification, engineering review, integration and handover as agreed. You can use
the toolkit without an engagement or a future product subscription.

## Agree the work before scaling it

The [manager packet](../examples/manager-review/README.md) includes a worked brief and copyable scope, review
and effort forms. These can support the first conversation without sending company records or installing tools.

1. **Assess one bounded problem.** Identify the recipient, unit/system, existing authoritative records, permitted
   access, intended decision and receiving format. Establish what is missing and whether a walkdown is needed.
2. **Agree scope and acceptance.** Specify deliverables, exclusions, responsible reviewers, source cut-off,
   schedule and a written price for that scope. A small paid assessment can precede a larger reconstruction.
3. **Deliver and review.** Retain source locators, corrections, disagreements and missing information. The
   responsible people accept the engineering interpretation and corrections through their existing processes.
4. **Exercise the next revision.** Introduce an agreed change, identify affected outputs, reconcile manual edits
   and hand back a new version while preserving earlier evidence. Ongoing updates can be scoped separately.

You retain your source records in agreed approved locations. Agree ownership and permitted use of engagement
outputs in writing; nothing is published as a case study without permission. Existing company systems retain
control of releases, access and accepted decisions.

## What the public examples establish

The [replacement handoff](../examples/maintenance-handoff/README.md) is fictional. The
[photo-to-record exercise](../examples/photo-to-record/README.md) uses two attributed public photographs.
The [CAD exchange](../examples/cad-handoff/README.md) tests a declared DXF profile, separately from proprietary
intelligent-CAD projects. These let you inspect the work before discussing your plant.

No plant engagement, operator acceptance, human review-time saving or general CAD round trip is established by
those examples. The [acceptance protocol](ACCEPTANCE.md) defines the observations a real engagement should retain.
