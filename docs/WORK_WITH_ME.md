# Work with me on a plant information problem

If maintenance records disagree, drawings are missing, or an engineering question takes too long to reconstruct,
start with one equipment system and one decision someone needs to make.

The work should also leave a foundation your team can maintain after the
assignment: usable asset identities, traceable sources, reviewed context, open
questions, ownership and revision history. As continuing use needs managed
maintenance, auditability and single-source-of-truth controls, that foundation
can support a scoped transition to ChangePilot or another suitable tool. Agree
the receiving mappings, authority, refresh responsibility and acceptance checks
for that destination using the [foundation roadmap](ENGINEERING_FOUNDATION.md).

[Connect with me on LinkedIn and message me there](https://www.linkedin.com/in/steve-vitale/).
Describe the problem, the kinds of records you have and who needs the result. Keep company documents private;
the first conversation does not require an upload or a GitHub issue.

## A first paid assignment: one system, one decision, one review package

**Maintenance and drawing information review.** Use this before a pump replacement, a small modification,
or acceptance of a consultant's returned drawings. Agree a fixed assessment fee for the selected records
and deliverables before work begins. Larger correction, walkdown or drawing packages can be quoted from
what the assessment finds. You do not need to wait for a software product launch.

An example starting scope to agree is one system, up to three P&ID sheets, one equipment export with up
to 25 selected assets, one set of markups/returned drawings when available, and one correction revision.
These are proposed scope limits, not a published price or delivery-time promise. Choose the smaller
subset that answers the actual question; a document-only or equipment-record job is also useful.

You receive:

- A readable decision brief with the reviewed scope, source revisions and the facts that remain unknown.
- A source-linked asset/record comparison and, where prepared connection records support it, a qualified
  cross-sheet route. Disputed links stay visible rather than becoming assumed connections.
- A correction and clarification queue with requested action, evidence, intended owner and receiving check.
- A review meeting and one agreed returned-record check. Earlier evidence and outstanding items remain.

Completion means the agreed records and requests have been accounted for, findings can be reconstructed,
and the recipient has the next actions. It need not mean every missing field has been discovered or every
engineering issue resolved. The client retains engineering release and source-system acceptance.
Use the [scope and acceptance template](../examples/consulting-start/SCOPE.md) to make that distinction explicit.

Where the job crosses maintenance and drawing records, the
[foundation handoff](FOUNDATION_HANDOFF.md) demonstrates the portable result and
next-change check using fictional inputs. Agree the client's authoritative homes,
identity mappings, permitted evidence, receiving owner and next refresh trigger in
the assignment. Qualify a product destination separately; local software checks
do not establish client acceptance, savings or an already available integration.

For an agreed package-unit or automation handoff, an optional
[requirements review worksheet](../examples/consulting-start/AUTOMATION_HANDOFF.md) separates requested
behavior, supplier responses and receiving evidence. Scope the interpretation and responsible reviewers
before promising this extension. The [platform comparison](COMPETITIVE_WORKFLOWS.md) helps assess an
existing vendor tool or specialist alongside the toolkit.

A separately scoped [calculation review](../examples/consulting-start/CALCULATION_REVIEW.md) can use a
suitable existing numerical tool, with sourced inputs, explicit assumptions, independent checks and an
editable handoff. Agree domain competence, validation and the receiving job before including it; the
maintenance/drawing review remains a complete starting assignment without simulation.

## Agree the work before scaling it

The [manager packet](../examples/manager-review/README.md) includes a worked brief and copyable scope, review
and effort forms. These can support the first conversation without sending company records or installing tools.

1. **Assess one bounded problem.** Identify the recipient, unit/system, existing authoritative records, permitted
   access, intended decision and receiving format. Establish what is missing and whether a walkdown is needed.
2. **Agree scope and acceptance.** Specify deliverables, exclusions, responsible reviewers, source cut-off,
   schedule and a written price for that scope. A small paid assessment can precede a larger reconstruction.
3. **Deliver and review.** Retain source locators, corrections, disagreements and missing information. The
   responsible people accept the engineering interpretation and corrections through their existing processes.
4. **Exercise the next revision.** Introduce an agreed change, identify affected outputs, reconcile manual edits
   and hand back a new version while preserving earlier evidence. Ongoing updates can be scoped separately.

You retain your source records in agreed approved locations. Agree ownership and permitted use of engagement
outputs in writing; nothing is published as a case study without permission. Existing company systems retain
control of releases, access and accepted decisions.

## What the public examples establish

The [replacement handoff](../examples/maintenance-handoff/README.md) is fictional. The
[photo-to-record exercise](../examples/photo-to-record/README.md) uses two attributed public photographs.
The [CAD exchange](../examples/cad-handoff/README.md) tests a declared DXF profile, separately from proprietary
intelligent-CAD projects. These let you inspect the work before discussing your plant.
The [three-sheet review](../examples/pid-path-review/README.md) shows actual source-qualified path checks,
an unrestricted comparison and a subsequent source correction on exposed fictional schedules.

No plant engagement, operator acceptance, human review-time saving or general CAD round trip is established by
those examples. The [acceptance protocol](ACCEPTANCE.md) defines the observations a real engagement should retain.

## From a first review to repeat work

If the recipient can use the first package, a follow-on scope can reconcile the selected CMMS records,
work through a drawing backlog, or repeat the same receiving check at the next project milestone.
Agree the records, revision cycle, review capacity, response expectations and fee for that work.
Existing approved Copilot/SharePoint access and ordinary files may be sufficient; a custom connector,
live alert service or platform migration is a separate job. The toolkit and self-service examples remain
usable without hiring me or subscribing to a future product.
